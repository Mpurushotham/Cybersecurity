# 🧩 Security Frameworks (NIST, ISO 27001, ISO 21434, etc.)

This document provides an overview of widely adopted cybersecurity frameworks and standards. These frameworks provide guidelines, best practices, and compliance benchmarks for securing information systems and aligning with regulatory requirements.

---

## 🔐 1. NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

### ✅ What It Is:
A risk-based framework developed by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) to help organizations identify, protect, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity threats.

### 📚 Core Functions:
- **Identify**: Asset management, risk assessments
- **Protect**: Access control, awareness training
- **Detect**: Anomalies and events
- **Respond**: Incident response planning
- **Recover**: Recovery planning, improvements

### 🧰 Use Case:
Adopted by both private and public organizations to structure their security programs and improve resilience.

---

## 🔐 2. NIST 800-53 / NIST 800-171

### ✅ What It Is:
Control catalogs that define security requirements for federal information systems (800-53) and protecting controlled unclassified information (CUI) in non-federal systems (800-171).

### 📚 Key Controls:
- Access Control (AC)
- Audit and Accountability (AU)
- System and Communications Protection (SC)
- Risk Assessment (RA)

---

## 📘 3. ISO/IEC 27001

### ✅ What It Is:
An international standard for managing information security through the implementation of an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

### 📚 Core Elements:
- Risk management process
- Security policies
- Asset management
- Supplier relationships

### 🧰 Use Case:
Organizations implement ISO 27001 to demonstrate security maturity, often for business or compliance reasons (e.g., GDPR).

---

## 🚗 4. ISO/SAE 21434 – Road Vehicles Cybersecurity

### ✅ What It Is:
A standard focused on cybersecurity risk management for electrical and electronic (E/E) systems in road vehicles, throughout the lifecycle (design, development, production, operation).

### 📚 Focus Areas:
- Threat analysis and risk assessment (TARA)
- Secure development lifecycle
- Post-production incident handling
- Governance and compliance for OEMs and suppliers

### 🧰 Use Case:
Mandatory in the automotive industry (linked with UNECE WP.29 R155/R156).

---

## 🛡️ 5. CIS Controls (Center for Internet Security)

### ✅ What It Is:
A set of 18 prioritized security best practices to block or mitigate known attacks.

### 📚 Examples:
- Inventory and Control of Assets
- Secure Configuration of Software
- Account Management
- Incident Response Management

### 🧰 Use Case:
Lightweight and practical framework for SMBs or organizations without formal GRC programs.

---

## 🧭 6. COBIT (Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies)

### ✅ What It Is:
A governance and management framework for enterprise IT, with a strong emphasis on aligning IT and business goals.

### 📚 Domains:
- Evaluate, Direct and Monitor (EDM)
- Align, Plan and Organize (APO)
- Build, Acquire and Implement (BAI)
- Deliver, Service and Support (DSS)
- Monitor, Evaluate and Assess (MEA)

---

## ✅ 7. PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)

### ✅ What It Is:
A set of security standards designed to protect cardholder data.

### 📚 Key Requirements:
- Install and maintain a firewall
- Encrypt transmission of cardholder data
- Regularly test security systems

---

## 📌 Comparison Table

| Framework        | Industry Focus           | Key Strengths                              | Common Use Case                          |
|------------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| NIST CSF         | General, Government       | Risk-based, Flexible                       | High-level strategy and improvement       |
| NIST 800-53/171  | Federal/Contractors       | Detailed control sets                      | System-level security and compliance     |
| ISO 27001        | Global, General           | Certifiable, Risk-oriented                 | Formal ISMS and regulatory alignment     |
| ISO 21434        | Automotive                | Lifecycle-based, OEM/Supplier alignment    | Vehicle E/E system cybersecurity         |
| CIS Controls     | General, SMBs             | Practical, Quick to implement              | Baseline security for any organization   |
| COBIT            | IT Governance             | Business alignment, Metrics                | IT governance and auditing               |
| PCI-DSS          | Financial/Payment Systems | Cardholder data protection                 | Compliance for merchants and processors  |
